01 THE SHORT VERSION

From a wavy number
to a whole new world.

Games like Minecraft use procedural rules to create spaces worth exploring. Here, Perlin noise gives every point on a grid a height—and the color tells you what lives there.

01 / SAMPLE

Ask noise for a value.

Each vertex samples a smooth, repeatable noise field using its X and Z position.

02 / DISPLACE

Push the points up.

The sample becomes elevation. More amplitude makes the same hills rise higher.

03 / COLOR

Turn height into habitat.

Vertex colors map low ground to water and peaks to stone and snow—one field, many biomes.
